10. Current State (as-is)
General
Standard stock can be set at two levels: Account Item (customer group / Account ID) and Store Item (business partner / Partner Ref).
Each item record carries at least: ID, Account ID or Partner Ref, Default Code (product), Category, Standard Stock, Active, and Write Date.
Standard stock is used to govern and limit product distribution to business partners.
Field Experience
During a visit, the Store Leader inputs current stock at the business partner; the system compares it with standard stock.
If there is a shortfall (standard stock − input stock), the difference becomes the suggested order quantity for the Store Leader.
Pain Points
Maintenance is mostly per-item; there are 19K+ account items and 500K+ store items, making mass changes slow.
When a product is revamped (new packaging / new SKU replacing the old one), the user must manually deactivate the old item first, then add the new item and set it active — item by item.
This manual process is error-prone: old products may be left active, or replacements may be missed, causing wrong order suggestions.
Import/export and change history are limited, so mistakes are hard to detect and trace.
11. Desired State (to-be)
11.1 Website Functional Requirements
Epic: Standard Stock Management (Account Item & Store Item)
Description:
Enhance the Standard Stock setting experience in SFM Backoffice for both Account Item (customer group level) and Store Item (business partner level). Standard stock defines the target quantity of a product that should be held at an account or store, and is used to limit distribution and to drive the suggested order quantity during Store Leader visits (standard stock − input stock).
Both levels share the same core behavior — list, search, filter, add, edit, activate/deactivate, import/export — so requirements are defined once and applied to each level, with each level respecting its own key (Account ID for Account Item, Business Partner / Partner Ref for Store Item) and the user's data access scope.
To reduce manual effort and revamp-related errors, the enhancement adds bulk actions (bulk edit, bulk activate/deactivate), a guided bulk revamp flow (deactivate old items and add their replacements in one action), improved Excel import/export with validation, copy setting between accounts/stores, and an audit trail for all changes.
Objective:
To provide an easy, safe, and auditable way to maintain standard stock at both Account Item and Store Item levels so that product distribution stays controlled and order suggestions in the field remain accurate — including during product revamp transitions.
The objectives are:
Let authorized users manage standard stock for Account Item and Store Item from a clear, filterable list.
Provide bulk activate, deactivate, and edit so many items can be maintained at once within the user's data access scope.
Provide a guided bulk revamp flow to deactivate old products and add their replacements as active in a single confirmed action.
Improve Excel Import/Export with a clear template, structure validation, and row-level validation feedback.
Provide Copy Setting to reuse standard stock configuration from one account/store to another.
Ensure only active standard stock records are used by the mobile order-suggestion logic.
Provide an audit trail for create, edit, activate/deactivate, bulk action, import, and copy.
